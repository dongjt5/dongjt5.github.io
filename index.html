Privacy & Security
How do I reset my password?
Go to the login page and select "Forgot password?". Enter your email address and follow the instructions sent to your inbox.
How do I create an account?
Select "Sign Up" and enter your email address. Follow the verification steps to complete your account setup.
Can I change my email address?
Yes. Open Account Settings, choose your email address, and complete the verification process for the new address.
Why am I not receiving the verification code?
Check your spam or junk folder first. If the code still does not arrive, request a new code after a few minutes or contact support.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Open Subscription & Billing in your account settings and follow the cancellation instructions for your current plan.
The app is not working correctly. What should I do?
Restart the app, make sure you have the latest version installed, and check your internet connection. If the issue continues, contact support with details of the problem.
